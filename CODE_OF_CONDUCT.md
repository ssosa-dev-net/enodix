# Code of conduct

enodix is made for network engineers by network engineers. Everyone who takes
part, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the project lives,
is expected to make it a place where people can ask, help and disagree
without being made to feel small.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. People come with different
  experience, languages and backgrounds.
- Keep criticism about the work, specific and useful.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what goes in, and that
  a "no" is about the change, not the person.
- Leave out real passwords, keys, addresses and configurations of networks
  you do not own, in issues, logs and screenshots alike.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or attacks on someone's identity.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or flooding issues and discussions.
- Using the project to attack networks or systems you are not authorised
  to work on.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately, using the contact details on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Say what happened, where (a link if there is one) and who
was involved. Reports are kept confidential. For a security problem in the
software itself, use the private reporting described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests,
and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, in proportion to what
happened. This applies in every project space and when someone represents
the project elsewhere.
